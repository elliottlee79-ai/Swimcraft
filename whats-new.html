What's new

Latest demo update: 0.111.536, 8 October 2026

The newest changes are first, then everything that has changed since the first demo build (0.111.405). Thank you to everyone who fished it and told us what they found. See what's coming next on the roadmap.

New in 0.111.536: fixes from the bank

  • Pressing Throw or L could freeze the game on every venue. That is fixed, along with a rarer freeze in the middle of a bite. If a single frame ever fails to draw, the game now carries on instead of stopping.
  • L and G now start the feed aim where your mouse is, not in the middle of the screen.
  • Sea fishing: a float at Durdle Door no longer drifts out on the tide until it reaches the rocks. With the bail arm closed no line is let out at all, and the float swings sideways and settles.
  • Loose feed and groundbait can no longer be thrown into the sea, whether by L, G or the Throw button.
  • Venue creator: custom stock weights under 1 lb (or 1 kg) can now be typed, so small fish like bleak, gudgeon and minnow can be stocked on a custom range.
  • Thanks to Dave for finding most of these.

New in 0.111.530: Swimcraft in your language

  • The whole game can now be played in 20 languages: English, German, French, Spanish (Spain), Spanish (Latin America), Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Dutch, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish, Polish, Czech, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Korean.
  • Every screen is covered: menus, the tutorial, the tackle box and bait box, the fishing screen, catch cards, records, the venue creator and the species creator.
  • The first time you start the game it picks your Windows language. You can change it at any time from Language on the home menu.
  • The translations are new, so if a line reads wrong, press F8 and click it to tell us. Every report is read.
  • Small fix: the session tally no longer says "fishs".

New in 0.111.509: fixes

  • No fish weighs less than a dram any more. The very smallest fish could show as 0lb 0oz 0dr. Thanks to Dave for spotting it.
  • In the species creator, a photo chosen before you've named your fish can now be chosen again once you have.

New in 0.111.507: the tackle box and bait box

  • Both boxes have been rebuilt from the ground up. Every rod, reel, line, hook and bait now has its own picture, set out in sections by brand.
  • Gear you haven't unlocked yet is shown clearly, so you can see what's waiting for you as you level up.
  • Your loadout runs across the top of the box as a row of miniature cards, and empty slots show the gear that could go there.
  • Photographs of all five float types sit beside the float rig, and the feeder rig screen is laid out in two columns.
  • The sea rig screen now matches the carp rig screen: three sea rigs, each drawn, with a description of how it fishes.
  • A new bait: Pineapple Wafter. Match T Hatch is now called Match T Hatch Pop-up, which is what it always was.

New in 0.111.507: species creator

  • Create your own fish. Answer a short set of plain questions about where it lives and how it feeds, fights and bites, add a photo, and save it.
  • Submitting your fish to be added to the game for everyone arrives in Early Access.

New in 0.111.507: fishing and venues

  • Your tutorial progress is now saved to your account, so finishing a tutorial stays finished when you sign in again.
  • A fish that reaches a snag now throws the hook after about 10 seconds. Give it slack to get it moving again.
  • The seasons guide now shows sea bass as the UK fish it is, scarce in winter, instead of borrowing the American largemouth bass's seasons.
  • Saving a venue with a lot of painted lake bed no longer times out.
  • On rivers with many pegs, the first pegs are no longer hidden off the edge of the list.
  • The fishing panel can no longer get stranded with its header off the screen.
  • Test This Venue now tells you if your venue failed to save, instead of carrying on silently.
  • The "venues loaded" message now always clears.
  • A smaller download.

New in 0.111.478: fishing

  • Strike the moment your float goes under and you'll hook the fish. A quick reaction is no longer punished; striking on a small dip or knock still pulls the bait.
  • Realistic mode: a button on the fishing panel turns off every on-screen prompt, including STRIKE NOW, and each fish holds the bait for its own time before dropping it. Your choice is remembered.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Z to strike, B for the bail arm, L for loose feed, G for groundbait, R to wind in, T for the tackle box, Y for the bait box and M for sound. They're all on the Controls card.
  • After a cast, the bail arm is open when you're float fishing moving water, so the float trots, and closed on still water.
  • The landing net pole now runs straight up to the net.
  • You can remove your loose feed or groundbait from the bait box with the × on its pill.

New in 0.111.478: rivers

  • A new, slowest flow speed: Barely moving.
  • A Small water setting for each peg, which slows the flow right down on narrow rivers and small streams.

New in 0.111.478: venue creator

  • You can now fish all the way up to the far bank on photographs taken with the water high in the frame. A hidden limit was stopping casts about a third of the way up the picture.
  • The distance lines are named by the rod that reaches them, such as Float rod reach and Feeder rod reach on coarse waters, and Pier rod and Beach-caster reach on sea venues, plus a Close in line. Hover a line to see its distance.
  • A blue distance tint shows the perspective of your water in the Map step, deep at your feet and hazy at the far bank.
  • The Paint step now outlines your water's edge and islands, so you can see the boundary you're painting against.
  • Switching between the Map and Paint steps no longer loses a shape you were in the middle of drawing.

The fight

  • Hooked fish now head for cover, such as reeds, lilies and snags, so every fight plays out differently. Your job is to stop them getting there.
  • Each species fights in its own way, and fish close to their record fight harder.
  • Your drag setting really matters now. Set it right and you can land a fish heavier than your line.
  • Carp and tench run, and big bream draw the line away. You'll see the float or the line move before you strike.
  • Netting has been reworked, and carp waters now have a proper carp net.
  • Move the mouse to raise and lower the rod tip, and hold the right button to lift. The rod rests while you're cast out.
  • Every rod bends like its type: sea rods are stiff, match and feeder rods are soft.

Bites and swims

  • The four difficulty levels have been retuned. Easy waters give quick bites and Specimen waters make you work for them.
  • Line strength now counts. Heavy line for small, shy fish will cost you bites.
  • Fishing close to cover is rewarded, while open water is fair but slower.
  • The swim bar now shows how well your swim is really fishing.
  • Tiger nuts now catch big tench and bream, as well as chub and barbel.
  • Bites come as they should when you fish bigger hooks for carp.
  • Smaller sea hooks, sizes 4 and 6, are now available for whiting and flatfish.
  • Your rig now counts. Lead weight, hooklink material, hooklength and rig choice each affect how fish take your bait, so matching your rig to the fish, the bottom and the bait pays off. The Rigipedia explains every rig.

Tackle and the screen

  • The line-out panel tells you what your rig is sitting over, for example silt / reeds.
  • Floats are bigger and easier to see at range.
  • Opening your tackle or bait box no longer winds your line in.
  • The tacklebox in the Angler's Lodge can now set up a sea loadout.
  • When you level up, new gear is marked until you've had a look at it.
  • The venue page has a Fish today button, and the date and time are easier to find. The exact-time slider now really does set the time you start fishing at.
  • Catch cards name the exact bait you used, for example Maize rather than Sweetcorn.
  • Fish on catch cards and record boards are shown at their true size, with a subtle 3D effect.
  • The home screen has a news board, and Guides now links to the Rigipedia. Sponsored items on the board are labelled as sponsored.
  • Share my catch: a Share button on your catch card, on your own catches and personal bests, and on world records you hold. It saves a picture of the card, with a code that takes friends to Swimcraft on Steam, and copies a caption ready to post.
  • New drawn icons across the tacklebox, bait box and account menu, and the box menus are easier to read.

Venue creator

  • The stocking bar tells you in words how well stocked your water is for its difficulty.
  • Adding fish pictures shows a live preview, with a guide to making cut-outs.
  • The Monster size band has been folded into Big Specimen, and sizes stop at the national record.

Fixes

  • The rod guides no longer jump from one side of the rod to the other.
  • The rod no longer loops back on itself when a fish is near the net.
  • The last card of the fishing tutorial now works as it should.
  • The rig named on screen is always the rig in the water. Switching from a carp rig to a float no longer carries old settings with it.
  • Pop-up messages now appear in front of record cards instead of behind them.
