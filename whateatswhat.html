What Eats What

What each fish eats, how it finds its food and how it behaves, so you can pick the right bait for the fish you are after.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Fish judge a bait by what it is made of before anything else. Put a bait on the hook that a fish never eats and you will not catch it, however good your swim is. This page goes through the fish in Swimcraft by type, and the food lists are the same ones the fish use in the game.

1. How to read this page

WordWhat it means when you fish
Main foodWhat this fish is looking for. The best choice.
Will eatTakes it readily, just a little less keenly than its main food.
Now and thenPossible, but you will wait far longer for a bite.
NeverThis fish will not take it at all.

Preferred conditions are where a fish feeds best, not the only times it feeds. The water temperature is what counts: the seasons given are for a temperate climate, so read them the other way round in the southern hemisphere, and allow for a warmer or colder water than the one you know.

A fish that finds food by smell comes to a strong-smelling bait from further away. A fish that hunts by movement finds a bait that moves more easily, and on a river the current moves your bait for you, on the bottom as well as under a float.

2. Carp and bottom feeders

Big-mouthed, patient fish that root through the bed for food. They find it mostly by smell, so a baited patch counts for more than a moving bait.

Common Carp

Clever and cautious, especially when big. Patrols in small groups and will clear a baited spot, then come back for more.

Will eatShellfish · Meat
NeverFish · Lures
Finds foodMostly by smell
FeedsOn the bottom
Preferred conditionsWater 15 to 28°C, best about 22°C · Late spring to early autumn · Low light, dusk or deep water · Dawn, dusk and dull days · Coloured water
CurrentStill or slow water

4. Predators
