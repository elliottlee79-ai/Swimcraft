The Bait Creator's Guide

How to make a bait of your own, from a new flavour of boilie to a fruit nobody has fished with yet.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The bait creator asks three pages of questions about a bait. Fish do not read the name on the packet: they judge a bait by what it is made of, how it tastes and smells, how big it is, how it looks and how it moves. Your answers are exactly those things, so they decide which fish take your bait and how keenly. This page explains each question, and what a moderator checks before your bait goes into everyone's Bait box.

1. Draft, submitted, approved

Open Create a Bait from the main menu. Whatever you make is saved as a draft first, and a draft is yours alone. Your own baits are in the Your baits list at the top of the creator, so you can come back to any of them.

StateWhat it means
DraftOnly you can see it. Once every question is answered it is in your own Bait box straight away, and you can fish with it and change it as often as you like. Catches made on it before it is approved never count for records.
With the moderatorsYou have sent it in. It cannot be changed while it waits for a decision.
ApprovedIt appears in everyone's Bait box, in its row, marked with a *.
Sent backA moderator has left a note saying what to change. Fix it and send it again.

2. Start from one of our baits, or make a new kind

The quickest way to make a bait is the Shortcut box at the top of page 1. Pick the bait in the game that is most like yours and every answer is filled in from it. Then change only what is different.

A strawberry boilie is a good example: start from one of our boilies, change the name, the taste and the colour, and you are nearly done. Anything you leave as it was fishes exactly as the bait you started from.

Leave the shortcut on A brand-new kind of bait to describe something the game does not have yet: a fruit that falls from a tree, a plant a vegetarian fish grazes on, a grub nobody has thought of. A new kind of bait starts with every question empty.

Each peg on a venue lists the kinds of bait its fish are fished with, such as Boilies or Corn / Particle. A bait you start from one of ours counts as the bait it started from. A brand-new kind of bait counts by what it is made of: a fruit is on the list wherever a peg lists Corn / Particle, and a new grub wherever it lists Maggots / Casters.

3. Page 1: the bait

Name

What players will see in the Bait box

A short name, up to 40 letters. It cannot be the name of one of our baits or of another player's bait.

Description line

One line, like our baits have

Every bait in the Bait box has one line under its name saying what it is and what it is good for. Write yours the same way, in up to 70 letters: Sweet and oily, a summer carp bait.

Row

Which row of the Bait box it sits in

Pick one of our rows, such as Boilies or Particles, or choose A new row and name it, for example Fruit. A new row appears in the Bait box after ours.

Made of

The first thing a fish checks

Grains, seeds, fruit or plants; worms, grubs or insects; shellfish or crustaceans; fish; meat; paste, boilie or pellet; or nothing edible. A fish that never eats plants will not take a fruit however good it smells, and a fish that lives on worms will take a worm-like bait readily. Choose what the bait really is.

Where it can be used

Fresh water, sea, or both

A freshwater bait is not offered on a sea venue, and the other way round.

Photo

The bait on its own

A PNG or WebP with a clear background, up to 5 MB, showing just the bait. Until you add one, the Bait box shows a drawing.

4. Page 2: how a fish senses it

These are the answers fish actually use. Each one helps some fish and not others, so there is no single best answer: think about which fish you want to catch.

QuestionWhat to think about
What does it taste like?Fishy, Sweet/Fruity, Spicy/Savoury, Meaty/Salty, Creamy/Milky, Natural or Nutty/Earthy. Each fish likes some tastes more than others, and some tastes suit warm water and some cold. A second taste is optional.
How strong does it smell?Barely, Noticeable, Strong or Reeks. A fish that hunts by smell finds a strong bait from further away, but very strong baits make big fish warier.
Is it oily?Oily baits work best in warm water and add little in cold.
Does it move?Still, Wafts, Wriggles or Swims. Fish that hunt by movement find a moving bait more easily.
How big is it?Its size in millimetres. The page shows some of our baits for scale. Too big for a fish's mouth and that fish will not take it; small fish are put off by big baits.
How does it look?Its colour, how much it stands out (Natural, Bright or Fluoro) and whether it catches the light. Bright baits are found in coloured water but can spook wary fish in clear water.
Does it float or sink?Sinks, sinks slowly, pops up off the bottom, or floats on top. Only a bait that floats can be fished on the surface.

6. Which fish will take it?

Once your answers are complete, the last page shows each fish that lives where your bait can be used, and how keen it is on your bait in summer and in winter: Takes it readily, Takes it, Now and then or Never.

This is the game's own judgement, made with the same checks the fish make in the water, at a peg that otherwise suits each fish. It is compared with what that fish is usually caught on. Change an answer and the list changes with it, so you can see straight away what a bigger size or a stronger smell does.

"Never" is usually the bait's size or what it is made of: too big for that fish's mouth, or something it does not eat. A real swim adds everything this list leaves out, such as the depth, the weather, the time of day and how much feed is in the water.

7. Your bait in the Bait box, and its level

Player-made baits sit in the same rows as ours, marked with a *, and the Bait box says what the mark means. They are chosen, put on the hook and thrown in as loose feed exactly like ours.

Every bait has a level at which it opens. Until a moderator sets your bait's level by how well it fishes, it opens at the level of the bait you started from, or at level 1 for a brand-new kind of bait. A moderator may set its level when it is approved: a bait that catches big fish easily can expect a higher one.

8. What the moderators look for

  • A real bait. Something anglers use or could use, described as it is, and plausible in real fishing. A brand-new kind of bait is welcome; a bait that is perfect at everything is not. Try to be real.
  • Honest answers. A 20 mm boilie entered as a 2 mm pellet, or a plain bait that reeks, will come back.
  • A clear name and description line. Saying what the bait is, and nothing offensive.
  • A clean photo. The bait on its own, against a clear or transparent background.

If your bait is sent back, the note tells you what to change. Fix it, then send it in again.
