Roadmap

Updated September 2026

Where Swimcraft is heading. This is our plan, not a promise: things move as we learn from the people fishing the game, and nothing here has a date on it yet.

In the demo now

  • Build your own venue from a photograph of a real water, then fish it.
  • Coarse, carp and sea fishing, with float, feeder and ledger tackle.
  • Realistic mode: no on-screen prompts, and every fish holds the bait for its own time.
  • River flow, from barely moving to fast, with a small-water setting for tight swims.

Coming in Early Access

  • Create your own species and stock them in your venues. With this, any angler anywhere can bring their own waters and their own fish to Swimcraft.
  • Share your venues and fish other anglers' waters in a shared venue library.
  • Lure fishing for pike and perch, with retrieve speed in your hands.
  • More sea fishing: float fishing from the rocks, and multi-hook sea rigs.
  • Better river fishing: trotting, holding back, and a lead that has to hold bottom in the flow.
  • Smarter fish: water temperature and season change where they feed, and the biggest fish are the most wary.
  • Achievements, including Steam achievements.
  • Your angler's avatar beside your catches and records.
  • A new look for the tackle box and bait box, with tackle you can turn and inspect.

Further ahead

  • Matches and tournaments against other anglers.
  • Curated fishing video inside the game.
