Your swim.

Swimcraft turns a photo of the water you love into a venue you can fish. Stock it with your own catches, tie proper rigs, play every fish like it matters, and chase records against anglers everywhere.

Wishlist on Steam

The fish on the bank is the fish in the game

Cut out a photo of your PB and every one you land in your venue wears it. Your mates’ fish, your club’s fish, that one that got you into carp fishing.

Feel every run

Drag, bail arm, line out and strain, live on screen. Keep the pressure on and you’ll gain line. Push too hard and it’s gone.

Proper tackle, properly tied

Hair rigs, chods, spinners, method feeders, wagglers and sea rigs. Pick the hooklink, the lead set-up and the bait, and see it all on the diagram before you cast.

Land it. Claim it.

Peg, venue, national and world records, and a catch card for every fish worth shouting about. The top spot is one bite away.
