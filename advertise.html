What you get

  • A place on the board on Swimcraft's home screen, shown to every player each time they start the game.
  • Your logo, a short line of text, and a link. A click opens your website or shop in the player's browser.
  • Your advert carries a small Sponsored label, as UK advertising rules expect, so it never looks like our own news.
  • The board takes turns between a few items, changing about every eight seconds, and holds still while a player hovers over it.
  • Start and end dates if you want them, so a placement can run for a sale, a match or an open day.
  • Changes show up the next time a player starts the game, with no download needed.

Who sees it

Swimcraft is a fishing simulator for carp, coarse and sea anglers, where players turn photographs of their own swims into venues and fish them. The free demo is on Steam and itch.io, and the full game is heading to Steam Early Access.

It suits businesses anglers already use:

  • Tackle shops, online and on the high street
  • Fisheries, clubs and syndicates
  • Bait and groundbait makers
  • Fishing holidays and guides

What to send us

Logo
A PNG with a transparent background, at least 180 by 90 pixels. It shows small, so a simple mark reads best.
Your line
Up to about 60 characters. The board fits two short lines.
Link
The page you want players to land on.
Dates
When you'd like it to start and finish, if it's for something in particular.

Tackle brands

Real rods, reels and terminal tackle in the tacklebox, by name, are planned for Early Access. If you make tackle and would like to be part of that, talk to us early.
